I bring chairs.

We sit in the old man's room.

I place my chair directly above the place where he lies.

They sat, and while I answered cheerily, they chatted of familiar things.


I could feel myself getting pale.

I wished them gone.

[TELL THEM TO LEAVE.]

Tell them? Telling them is far too forceful.

[ASK THEM POLITELY TO LEAVE.]

No.

Only a madman would be so foolish.

And I am not a madman.

To ask them to leave in the middle of conversation would be suspicious.

[MY HEAD ACHES.]
