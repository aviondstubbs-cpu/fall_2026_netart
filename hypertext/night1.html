It is midnight.

The old man's door is closed.

My hand is steady.

First, the latch.

[UNLOCK IT.]

Gently, I unlocked the door.

There is no reason to hurry.

[CRACK THE DOOR.]

I opened the door just a crack.

[A LITTLE MORE.]

The door opens farther.

No more.

[LISTEN.]

The old man is sleeping.

[OPEN IT WIDER.]

I opened it enough for my head.

There is darkness.

Complete darkness.

The lantern is still covered.

[UNCOVER THE LANTERN.]

No.

Not yet.

[WAIT.]

I waited.

[CONTINUE WAITING.]

Longer.

[STILL WAITING.]

Yes. Still waiting.

I was very patient.

[WAIT.]

I waited for nearly an hour.

[UNCOVER THE LANTERN.]

The lantern revealed only a narrow beam.

Just enough to cross the room, to the bed, to the eye.

It is closed.

[WAKE THE OLD MAN.]

Why?

[DO IT ANYWAYS.]

Do what? Kill the old man?

No.

I cannot.

The eye is closed.

[LEAVE.]
