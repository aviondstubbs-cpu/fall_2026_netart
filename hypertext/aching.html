Something bothers me.

[WHAT?]

It is not a sound.

It is a pressure.

Within my ears.

[A HEADACHE.]

Exactly that.

Nothing more.

[IGNORE IT.]

I continue speaking.

The pressure within my ears turns to a faint ringing within my ears.

[IGNORE IT.]

I cannot.

Something is beating.

[HIS HEART.]

Impossible.

Only a madman would even suggest something so inept.

[MY HEART.]

Mine is beating.

That is not what I hear.

[CAN THEY HEAR IT?]

No.

[IGNORE IT.]
