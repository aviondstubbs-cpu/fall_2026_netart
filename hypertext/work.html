You may be expecting panic.

But only madmen panic. And I am not mad.

I simply worked to clean everything up. My hands were steady.

[THE BODY?]

I dismembered the old man's body. I cut off the head and the arms and the legs.

[THE ROOM?]

There was no blood to wash out. I had been too wary for that.

[THE FLOOR?]

I then took up three planks from the flooring of the chamber, and buried the old man underneath.

When I finish, there is a knock at the front door.
