My nerves?

Yes, I have already admitted to them.

That should count for something.

A madman would deny their nerves, even when it is obvious. I do not deny it. My hands shook when there was nothing to cause the shaking. Sleep came badly. Sudden sounds made me flinch harder than others.

But I cannot be mad. If you were to give me hands a task, they would steady without so much as a second thought.

The hands of madmen are uncertains. My hand was never an uncertain one. My hand was steady.
