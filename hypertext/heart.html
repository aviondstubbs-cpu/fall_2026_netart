I hear it.

[HEAR WHAT?]

The old man's Heart.

I hear it.

[LISTEN CLOSER.]

It is not my own Heart. I know what mine sounds like. It is the old man's.

[WAIT.]

And let the eye continue to stare at me?

I cannot.

I can let the Heart continue to beat no longer.

Someone else could hear it.

The neighbors, surely.

Someone could wake.

Someone could come.

There is not much time.

[STOP THE HEARTBEAT.]

[LEAVE.]

And let the old man cry out?

I cannot.

I can let the Heart continue to beat no longer.

The neighbors, surely.

Someone could wake.

Someone could come.

There is not much time.

[STOP THE HEARTBEAT.]

[IGNORE IT.]

And let the noise become unbearable?

I cannot.

I can let the Heart continue to beat no longer.

The neighbors, surely.

Someone could wake.

Someone could come.

There is not much time.
