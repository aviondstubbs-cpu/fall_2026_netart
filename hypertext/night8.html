It is midnight.

The old man's door is closed.

My hand is steady.

First, the latch.

[UNLOCK IT.]

Gently, I unlocked the door.

There is no reason to hurry.

[CRACK THE DOOR.]

I opened the door just a crack.

[A LITTLE MORE.]

The door opens farther.

My thumb slips.

And the old man stirs.

             "Who's there?" he says.
[ANSWER HIM.]

What would I say?

[DO NOT MOVE.]

[RUN.]

No.

[DO NOT MOVE.]

[DO NOT MOVE.]
