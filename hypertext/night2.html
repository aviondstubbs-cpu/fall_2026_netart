It is midnight again.

The old man's door is closed.

My hand is steady.

First, the latch.

[UNLOCK THE LATCH.]

Gently, I unlocked the door.

There is no reason to hurry.

[OPEN THE DOOR.]

I opened the door exactly as far as needed.

[LISTEN.]

The old man is sleeping.

[UNCOVER THE LANTERN.]

A thin beam.

The eye is closed.

[LOOK AT HIM.]

Without the eye, the old man is only an old man.

[LEAVE.]
