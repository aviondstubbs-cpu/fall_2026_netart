I hear a lot.

The disease had sharpened my senses — not destroyed — not dulled them.

I heard the noises of the house when everyone else slept through it.

I knew upon which step the old man had walked on without seeing him.

I could hear a watch through the cloth of a shirt.

I hear all things in Heaven and in earth and in hell.

I hear sounds everywhere. People who cannot hear them call the people who can mad.
