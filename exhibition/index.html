I Was Here. I Am Here. I’ll Be Here Tomorrow.

Curated by Avion S.

About the Exhibition

Life is often cruel. Things fall apart, people disappoint us, and the future is always uncertain. There isn’t a solution to all our problems, and no amount of optimism can guarantee that things will get better. But even when everything seems hopeless, there is still something worth holding onto: each other.

This exhibition is about carrying on, not because life is easy or because things are guaranteed to get better, but because continuing to move forward, despite everything, is meaningful in and of itself. Through three net artworks, Open Field, The Nicest Place on the Internet, and Wish, this exhibition hopes to show you how small gestures of kindness and creation can bring people, even complete strangers, together.

All three artworks use the internet to create a sense of community between people who may never meet. In Open Field, visitors leave behind drawings, images, and messages that slowly fade over time. The Nicest Place on the Internet is a site where visitors can receive and give “virtual hugs,” videos of people hugging their cameras, from strangers around the world. Finally, Wish lets visitors share their hopes and desires and encounter others' wishes.

Together, I think these artworks represent yesterday, today, and tomorrow. The traces of ourselves we leave behind, the kindness we receive in the present, and the hopes we carry into the future. None of these gestures will change the world. A drawing might disappear, a hug might only make someone feel better for a few seconds, and a wish might never come true. But I don’t think that makes them meaningless. In all three artworks, people choose to give something of themselves to strangers, whether it's their creativity, their kindness, or their hopes for the future. And I think that is what carrying on is really about.

And carrying on doesn't mean you have to keep moving forward every single day. Sometimes, you need to rest. Whether that's for a few minutes, days, months, or even years, taking a rest doesn't mean you've given up. You can always begin again.

You were here yesterday. You are here today. And you are going to be here tomorrow.

Artworks

Yesterday

Open Field (2026)

By Aaron Higgins

Open Field is a collaborative 3D canvas where visitors can draw, place images, and write text that others can see. They gradually fade over time, allowing the canvas to constantly change as people come and go.

Visit Open Field

Today

The Nicest Place on the Internet (2011)

By Jeff Lam and Lauren Perlow

The Nicest Place on the Internet is a website featuring videos of people from around the world giving virtual hugs to the visitor. You can also submit your own hugging videos to be added to the website.

Visit The Nicest Place on the Internet

Tomorrow

Wish (2021)

By Alvin Ruiyuan Zhong

Wish is a website where visitors type their wishes, putting them on the site for the people who make the next few wishes to see immediately after.
