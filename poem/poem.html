A Brief List of Objections

I know I don’t get much say anymore,
but this is absolutely not how I wanted to be handled.

Firstly, this venue is abhorrent.

  • The walls are too beige.
  • The carpet pattern is tacky.
  • That sad piano is killing me.

And that photo?
The one where I was caught off guard,
eating breakfast
at 4 in the morning.

Who picked that one out of all the photos of me?
And who was proud enough of it
to hang it above the entire place?
I look like I’ve got one foot in the grave already.

Another thing— why did you tell the truth?
“He died peacefully in his sleep.”
That’s way too played out for my liking.

Couldn’t you say I died
saving a runaway train
from a terrorist group?
Now that’s a cool way to go.

One last thing.
Don’t move on from me.
That’s lazy, and quite frankly,
downright disrespectful.

Mourn me properly:

  1. Mourn me daily.
  2. Bring me up mid-conversation.
